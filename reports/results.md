## Sensitivity: Polymarket markets that closed on schedule

10.0% of Polymarket markets closed more than a day before their scheduled end, usually because the event happened. Their close time depends on their outcome, so 'the price 1 day before close' is sampled at an outcome-dependent time, which biases it toward underpricing Yes.
