chapter 1

A price is a probability

On a prediction market, you buy a contract that pays $1 if something happens and nothing if it doesn't. So the price tells you how likely traders think it is: a contract trading at 30¢ means roughly a 30% chance.

example contract

Will it rain in Toronto tomorrow?

Tap a side to see what you'd be betting.

If those prices are honest, then across every 30¢ contract, about 30% should pay out. A market where that holds at every price is called calibrated. That's what I tested, using each market's price 30 days, 7 days, 1 day and 1 hour before it closed. (The final price is useless: by then everyone knows the answer.)
